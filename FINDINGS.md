# Findings

# Executive Summary

The purpose of this assessment was to identify the correct FY2026 Q2 net revenue and determine why Finance no longer trusted the warehouse.

The original pipeline reported:

**FY2026 Q2 Net Revenue: 727,922.09 USD**

After investigating the data model, business rules, and SQL pipeline, I identified several material defects affecting revenue calculations.

After implementing the highest-impact fixes, the pipeline produced:

**FY2026 Q2 Net Revenue: 382,929.62 USD**

Difference:

**344,992.47 USD (-47.39%)**

The final solution improved the test suite from:

**4/7 passing → 7/7 passing**

---

# Defect 1 - Order Duplication

## What was wrong

The fact table joined orders directly to `raw_order_items`.

This caused a single order to appear multiple times whenever it contained multiple items.

## How I found it

I compared:

- COUNT(*) from `fact_order`
- COUNT(DISTINCT order_id)

Results:

- fact_order rows: 174,089
- distinct orders: 109,018

## Impact

The join inflated revenue calculations and broke the expected grain of one row per order.

## Fix

Removed the join with `raw_order_items` and maintained one row per order.

# Defect 2 - Pending Orders Included in Revenue

## What was wrong

Orders with status `pending` were included in revenue calculations.

## How I found it

Reviewing the Revenue Policy and validating order status distributions.

## Impact

Pending orders contributed:

**8,248.81 USD**

to FY2026 Q2 revenue.

This represented approximately:

**1.13% of reported revenue.**

## Fix

Restricted the fact table to:

```sql
WHERE order_status = 'completed'
```

# Defect 3 - Internal Test Accounts Included

## What was wrong

Internal test accounts were included in reported revenue.

## How I found it

Review of player domains revealed:

```text
twinhearth.test
```

These accounts generated significant purchasing activity.

## Impact

Revenue generated by test accounts:

**124,264.88 USD**

Approximately:

**17.07% of reported FY2026 Q2 revenue.**

## Fix

Excluded:

```sql
pl.email_domain <> 'twinhearth.test'
```

# Defect 4 - FX Rate Lookup Logic

## What was wrong

Exchange rates were matched only on exact dates.

## How I found it

39,001 orders had:

```sql
rate_to_usd IS NULL
```

The Revenue Policy requires using the latest available rate on or before the order date.

## Impact

Thousands of rows produced null monetary values and incorrect revenue calculations.

## Fix

Replaced exact-date matching with:

```sql
rate_date <= order_date
ORDER BY rate_date DESC
LIMIT 1
```

This allows orders placed on weekends and non-business days to use the most recent available exchange rate.

---

# Defect 5 - Historical Platform Fees

## What was wrong

The pipeline always used the latest fee available for a platform regardless of order date.

## How I found it

The source table contained:

- effective_from
- effective_to

but the SQL ignored both fields.

## Impact

Historical revenue was calculated using incorrect platform fees.

## Fix

Applied fee schedules based on validity periods using:

```sql
effective_from <= order_date
```

and

```sql
effective_to >= order_date
```

---

# Defect 6 - Refund Logic

## What was wrong

Refund calculations relied on:

```sql
order_status = 'refunded'
```

instead of using the refunds table.

## How I found it

The Revenue Policy explicitly states:

> The refunds table is the system of record.

The source data contained:

- 7,693 refunds
- 4,829 partial refunds
- goodwill credits
- price adjustments

## Impact

Partial refunds may not be fully reflected in revenue calculations.

## Fix Status

Identified and documented.

A complete refund refactor was not implemented within the available time after prioritizing higher-impact defects.
